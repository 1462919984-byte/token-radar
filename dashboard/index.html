The value model

implied_value_usd = comps_value_usd × token_odds

Comps value fits the market’s TVL→market-cap curve with plain log-log ordinary least squares on the 128 positive market caps, then applies it to every launch candidate. Token odds is a fitted logistic chance the protocol records a token. Both are fit at run time from the snapshot’s own figures — no hardcoded names. Unlisted protocols are imputed the median age of the token-bearing set.
